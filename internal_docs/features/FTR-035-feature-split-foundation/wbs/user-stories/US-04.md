### US-04

- **Phase ID:** US-04
- **Type:** user-story
- **Source UC:** UC-04
- **Title:** As a user, I want a confirmed split applied atomically, as an initial split or a late child, so that the epic record, child folders and registry stay consistent
- **Commit type:** feat
- **Commit scope:** —
- **Commit subject:** implement US-04 atomic apply

**Context:**

This phase adds the apply operation that turns a confirmed proposal into an epic record, child folders and registry entries. It works as an initial split or as a late child. It builds on the registry helpers, slug rule, dependency order and BR-03 warnings from earlier phases. The pure builders (IDs, documents, registry content) come first. They are followed by the staged-move-and-rollback engine and the orchestration that joins them. The tests come last.
